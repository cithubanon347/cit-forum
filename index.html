Community Protocol & Terms

Before entering or establishing your profile, you must review and agree to the platform guidelines:

1. Truth & Accountability Absolute expression autonomy does not grant immunity from facts; spreading deliberate false information or defamatory rumors is strictly forbidden.
2. No Baseless Gossip Targeted gossip, harassment, or malicious call-outs directed at individuals or external student groups without valid context will result in immediate removal of the post.
3. Liability Waiver & Security In the hypothetical event of an institutional or administrative breach, the website creator holds zero legal liability or responsibility. Architecture is designed to secure your identity unless compromised by your own handle choices.
4. Strict Pseudonymity Mandate Never utilize your legal name, registration number, college email handle, or standard social aliases tied to your real identity.
5. Zero Doxxing Policy Publishing private phone numbers, personal photographs, confidential academic records, or leaked screenshots of private chats will result in an instant permanent ban.
6. Spam & Automation Control Flooding discussion channels with repetitive text payloads, bot scripts, or external commercial ads is prohibited.
7. Executive Moderation Authority Platform moderators and administrators reserve full authority to purge violating posts, lock threads, or revoke user access at discretion.
8. Credential Ownership You are solely responsible for saving your security question answers and password. Lost credentials cannot be manually recovered by administrators.
9. External Link Restrictions Posting malicious IP loggers, malware links, or unauthorized external redirects will trigger immediate content removal.
10. Autonomous Status This platform is an independent unofficial student sanctuary operating strictly outside official college administration oversight.
